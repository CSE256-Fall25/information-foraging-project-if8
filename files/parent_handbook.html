Welcome & Philosophy

Learn about our play-based learning approach and how we partner with families to create a nurturing environment for every child's unique developmental journey.

Staff & Curriculum

Our experienced teachers provide individualized support and develop curriculum based on each child's needs, incorporating math, language, and other learning areas into hands-on play experiences.

Classroom Information & Scheduling

Age-appropriate programs for Two's/Young Three's, Three's/Young Four's, and Four's/Young Five's. Includes daily school hours, Bear Tracks extended day program times, and full-day options for working families.

Parent-Staff Communication

Communication is ongoing throughout the year. Teachers and staff provide updates via newsletters, the school website, and emails to inform parents about current themes, classroom activities, and upcoming events.

If you have concerns or questions about your child’s adjustment or progress, teachers commonly reach out by phone or email. Parents are also encouraged to contact a teacher or the director directly via email or phone. Teachers can be reached after the school day for personalized conversations.

Assessment & Parent/Teacher Conferences

Details on orientation days to support transitions into school, ongoing parent-teacher communication methods, developmental assessments, and twice-yearly parent-teacher conferences to discuss your child's progress and kindergarten readiness.

Discipline and Classroom Approach

At WUNS, teachers create a positive, nurturing environment while helping children manage their own behavior. Classroom rules keep children, peers, and toys safe, while allowing teachers to focus on engaging activities. Teachers anticipate challenges, guide children toward positive behaviors, acknowledge feelings, and use encouragement and simple, immediate consequences when needed. Reflection and discussion help children consider alternative actions and resolve conflicts constructively. Teachers also work with parents if behaviors continue to ensure every child, including spirited or active children, receives the support they need to thrive in the classroom.

Family events including school picnics, open house, Halloween parade, parent socials, Donuts with Dads, Muffins with Moms, and our annual Night at the Magic House.
