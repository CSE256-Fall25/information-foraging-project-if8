Application Procedure

Parents interested in Washington University Nursery School should contact the school to schedule a visit. To apply, you must complete an application form, which only needs to be filled out once. You may fill out the Application for Admission (Online) or print and return the PDF Version. An application fee of $40 is required. After your application and fee are received, your child is placed on an enrollment list. You will be notified of acceptance in the March preceding the fall enrollment date.

For Parents Whose Child Has Been Accepted:

Congratulations! Please follow these steps to finalize enrollment:

  1. Enroll & Submit Forms
  2. Pay the non-refundable deposit (1 month’s tuition): Tuition Info
  3. Complete any required medical or developmental history forms
